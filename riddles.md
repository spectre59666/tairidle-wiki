Considering the ruins being a sacred chamber, maybe the legends of some dormant *<u>Titans</u>* in here are true ?
Maybe the researchers could help you. Some of them would even say *"There's a recurring image in the murals — something long and flat, finned. Ancient. We can't identify the species."* or *"The carvings near the lower hall depict what looks like a deep-sea creature. In a landlocked mountain ruin. Curious"*
*"One motif appears forty-seven times on the sealed wall. It looks like a fish, but nothing alive today matches it exactly."* 
It seems there is also a part of the riddle that use some kind of *alphabet*.

<summary> <b>Hint 1</b> : </summary>

</details>

<details>
<summary> <b>Hint 3</b> :</summary>

## The Desert :
The next riddle is very slit and sandy, it says *"Beyond the door stretches a vast desert of chuming, raging sand — the winds are ferocious and impassable on foot. It looks as though only a Pokémon that commands the sand itself could make it safe to venture through."*
That sounds like one hell of a sandstorm.

<details>

## Flower Garden :
This one is easier than it seems ! 
As you can see there is two items that will supposedly let you access this fancy area, or is there ?
Something that comes from the *<u>Sun</u>* , along with the *<u>scent</u>* of delicates flowers. 
The riddle says *"A blinding light floods in the from the western door. The air carries the scent of lsuh greenery and riot of flowers — beautiful, but overwhelmingly intoxicating. You feel lightheaded from the smell, and you know you'll need a Pokémon that can help you push through the overpowering scent."*

<details>
<summary> <b>Hint 1</b> : </summary> 
- Some pokémons evolves with the <u>Sun</u>, and may be related to it.
</details>

<details>
<summary> <b>Hint 2</b> : </summary>
- You need to have one Pokémon that <u>evolves</u> via <u>Sun Stone</u> (E.g. Sunflora, Bellossom, Whimsicott, Lilligant, or Heliokisk.) And teach him <u>Sunny Day</u>
</details>

<details>
<summary> <b>Hint 3</b> : </summary> 
- The <u>aromatic scent</u> of flowers may open help you breathe through the garden.
</details>

<details>
<summary> <b>Hint 4</b> : </summary>
- At last, you need a pokémon, or the same one, to learn <u>Sweet Scent</u> to be able to enter the <b><u>Flower Garden</u> ! Welcome !</b> 
</details>
